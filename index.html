Maven Toys – Sales & Inventory Analysis

End-to-end data analytics project | SQL Server & Power BI

Using SQL Server and Power BI, I developed a data warehouse, performed data cleaning and transformation, designed a relational data model and created DAX measures to analyse a fictional toy retailer.

Key findings

Insight 01 · 2022

Revenue vs. profitability

In August 2022, the lowest monthly revenue ($490K) coincided with the highest gross profit margin (32.47%). Lower revenue does not necessarily translate into lower profit margins.

Insight 02 · 2023

Growth and margin compression

Revenue increased by 22.88%, while gross profit grew by only 12.44%, a gap of 10.44 percentage points. A shift towards lower-margin products drove a 2.48 percentage-point decline in gross profit margin. Electronics achieved the highest margin (41.09%), while Toys generated the highest gross profit despite the lowest margin (20.75%).

Insight 03 · 2024

A return to balanced growth

Revenue and gross profit grew by 5.58% and 5.84%, respectively. Gross profit margin remained stable at 26.85%. Both 2023 and 2024 experienced a late-summer slowdown followed by a recovery towards December.

Insight 04 · 2024

Store performance

27 of 50 stores (54%) operated below the overall gross profit margin of 26.85%. Several below-average revenue stores nevertheless achieved margins above 30%. Ciudad de Mexico, Guadalajara and Monterrey were the leading revenue-generating cities.

Insight 05 · 2022–2024

Product performance and sales mix

The top three products generated approximately 33.3% of gross profit, with Colorbuds alone contributing 17.8%. Between 2022 and 2023, Magic Sand gained 9.2 percentage points in revenue share while Colorbuds lost 8.2. Magic Sand achieved a margin of only 12.51%, compared with 53.37% for Colorbuds.

Insight 06 · Inventory snapshot

Inventory performance and stock risk

Three of 35 products (8.57%) were at risk of stockout, with fewer than seven days of inventory remaining based on recent demand. $4.83K (1.61% of total inventory value) was tied up in inactive stock. The coexistence of inactive inventory and stockout risk highlights a mismatch between inventory and demand.
